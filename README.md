# KNGS KS3 Cover Lesson Hub

A bank of 20 standalone 55-minute cover lessons for Years 7, 8 and 9.

**Student website:** https://kingsnortongirls.github.io/cover/

**Main CS homepage:** https://kingsnortongirls.github.io/homepage/

A direct link to a particular lesson can be shared in Teams or Edulink, e.g. https://kingsnortongirls.github.io/cover/?lesson=05

## Lesson structure

- 5 min: automatically marked Do Now vocabulary quiz.
- 10 min: self-contained reading and clearly defined key terms.
- 15 min: structured research with direct links to specific webpages.
- 20 min: substantive written task, four success criteria, extra support, worked example and stretch challenge.
- 5 min: exit ticket and completion check.

The internal reading and definitions allow a lesson to continue if external sites are blocked.

## Using the hub for cover

1. Cover staff choose a lesson and click **Copy link**.
2. Pupils enter full name and year group to start or resume.
3. Pupils answer the guided questions and complete the main task. **Extra support** includes LPA-friendly sentence starters, a short worked example and task breakdown.
4. Pupils use **Print / Save as PDF** for evidence, or **Download work (JSON)** for an editable backup.
5. Pupils can use **Import saved work** for the *same* name, year and lesson on another device. **Blank worksheet** offers a printable paper backup.

## Editing and publishing

- `lessons.js` is the editable bank of all 20 lessons, vocabulary, source URLs and prompts.
- `index.html` supplies the responsive lesson hub, quiz, saving and printing.
- `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` deploys to GitHub Pages from `main` and attempts to enable Pages automatically.

If publishing fails, inspect the repository's **Actions** tab and **Settings → Pages**.

## Safeguarding and privacy

Pupil work is saved only in that browser's local storage and is separated by lesson, typed name and year. Shared computers are not private; pupils should print or download their evidence before leaving. No online student account or remote response collection is used. Never enter passwords or sensitive personal information. Cover staff should confirm that recommended pages pass the school's filtering rules.

The Research Skills Passport is intentionally excluded. Long-form answers are **not** automatically marked.
